CONSTRUCTION NOTES

From five grids
to a Penrose tiling.

The pentagrid lives in one plane; its dual tiling lives in another. A cell of the grid becomes a vertex of the tiling, and a crossing of two lines becomes a rhombus.

The following construction uses the ceiling convention in the poster. Choose Side by side above to follow the correspondence as you change a shift.

  6. 06

    Read the finite patch

    The app includes crossings inside a disk of radius R in grid space. Its projection gives a finite, roughly circular patch. Increasing the radius constructs more tiles; zooming changes only the view.

    A finite patch has boundary effects, so the displayed ratio need not be exactly the golden ratio. Non-periodicity refers to the whole infinite tiling: no nonzero translation preserves it, although finite patches recur.

    What happens at a singular grid?

    If three or more lines meet, there is no unique four-cell prescription at that crossing. The app detects such coincidences within the sampled patch and applies a tiny, reproducible perturbation that preserves the zero sum. This chooses a nearby regular grid. A notice shows the effective offsets, which are also included in the geometry export.
